## 1. Voice fingerprint

- Sentence rhythm: short, declarative, builder-direct. One idea per line. No hedging.
- Signature openers: lead with the category, not the tool — "The best prices in US real estate are set at foreclosure and tax deed auctions."
- Punctuation habits: plain. Few em dashes.
- Words and phrases you use a lot: auction, tax deed, foreclosure, per door, county record, in the trenches, unfair advantage, outbid the guesswork
- Words and phrases you NEVER use: "Know your number before the gavel" (retired), "Shapira Max Bid" (retired), dread or fear framing, "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world"
- Emoji: none, or one at most

- Always: every figure comes from references/story-bank.md with its source. No source, no number.
- Never: name any other person (family, partners, clients, competitors' staff).
- Never: describe the founder as a licensed general contractor.
- Never: mention patents, internal methodology names, financing behind any sale, or internal tools/vendors.
- Never: homeowner-facing or foreclosure-relief messaging ("save your home", "stop foreclosure"). Audience is buyers, not distressed owners.
- Never: engagement bait ("comment YES"), fake polls, pods.
